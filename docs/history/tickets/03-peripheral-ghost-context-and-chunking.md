# 03 Peripheral Ghost Context and Chunking

## What to build
Multi-mode cognitive reading presentations to reduce eye strain and comprehension loss. In Peripheral Ghost Context mode, the viewport displays the immediately preceding word to the left and upcoming word to the right at 15–25% opacity while the active word remains centered and focused at the ORP. In Multi-Word Chunking mode, the engine displays 2 or 3 words simultaneously as a cohesive thought group.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Users can toggle between three display modes: Classic ORP, Ghost Context, and Chunking.
- [x] Ghost Context mode displays previous word and next word flanking the active word with low opacity and smooth transitions.
- [x] Chunking mode supports 1, 2, or 3 words per burst with the primary focal anchor aligned.
- [x] Switching modes recalculates viewport positioning seamlessly without breaking playback cadence.
