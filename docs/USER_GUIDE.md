# Focus Reader Pro — User Guide

Welcome to **Focus Reader Pro**! This guide walks you through every feature, customization setting, and keyboard shortcut to help you accelerate your reading speed and comprehension.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Ingesting Text

Focus Reader Pro provides four easy ways to start reading:

### Option A: Paste Custom Text
1. Click the text box in the **Setup View**.
2. Paste any article, notes, or essay text.
3. The live word counter and estimated reading time will update automatically.
4. Click **Start Reading** or press `Ctrl + Enter`.

### Option B: Drag and Drop Files
- Drag any `.txt`, `.md`, `.epub`, or `.pdf` file from your desktop directly onto the upload dropzone.
- The app automatically extracts the text and saves the document to your local **Document Library**.

### Option C: File Picker
- Click the **Browse** button in the dropzone to select a file from your computer.

### Option D: Try the Demo
- Click **Try Demo** in the top header to immediately load our curated speed-reading scientific primer.

---

## 2. Choosing Your Reading Mode

Focus Reader Pro features three distinct RSVP visualization modes:

| Mode | Key | Best For | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Classic ORP** | `1` | Maximum Velocity | Displays one word at a time, centering the **Optimal Recognition Point (ORP)** letter in crimson directly between the vertical guide lines. |
| **Ghost Context** | `2` | Natural Scanning | Displays the active word centered, flanked by dimmed "ghost" previews of the previous and upcoming words for ambient peripheral awareness. |
| **Chunking** | `3` | Advanced Readers | Flashes words in rhythmic multi-word bursts (1, 2, or 3 words at a time) to train phrase-level recognition. |

---

## 3. Playback Controls & Navigation

### Transport Controls
- **Play / Pause:** Press `Space` or click the central Play button.
- **Restart Stream:** Press `R` or click the circular reload icon.
- **Exit Reader:** Press `Escape` or click the **Back** button to return to the setup screen.

### Stepping & Scrubber
- **Step 1 Word:** Press `←` (Previous) or `→` (Next) while paused.
- **Sentence Rewind / Skip:** Press `[` to jump back to the beginning of the previous sentence, or `]` to jump forward.
- **Interactive Scrubber:** Click or drag anywhere along the timeline bar at the bottom to jump to any percentage of the document.
- **Pause Context Card:** Whenever you pause playback, an ambient preview card appears below the viewport showing the full surrounding sentence.

---

## 4. Customizing Your Reading Experience

Open the **Settings Drawer** (gear icon in the top right) to configure:

### Velocity & Progressive Acceleration
- **Target WPM:** Adjust speed between 50 and 1500 words per minute using the slider or preset pills.
- **Progressive Speed:** Ramp speed smoothly up to your target over your chosen number of words (e.g. start at 250 WPM and ramp up to 600 WPM over 50 words).
- **Smart Punctuation:** Automatically adds proportional pauses at sentence terminators (`.`, `!`, `?`), clause breaks (`,`, `;`), and paragraph transitions to give your brain time to assimilate ideas.

### Typography & Focus
- **Font Family:** Choose from **Inter Sans** (modern clean), **JetBrains Mono** (fixed-width code), **OpenDyslexic** (weighted bottoms designed to reduce letter confusion), or **System Default**.
- **Font Scale:** Select between Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large.
- **Bionic Reading Mode:** Emphasizes the initial characters of each word in bold, guiding the eye's fixation automatically.
- **Focal Guide:** Press `F` to toggle the center vertical focal lines on or off.
- **Themes:** Switch between sleek OLED Dark Mode and clean Minimalist Light Mode.

### Auditory Metronome
- Press `M` or toggle the **Audio Cadence** switch to enable low-latency synthesized micro-clicks that pulse in lockstep with every word flash. You can customize the click pitch and volume in Settings.

---

## 5. Document Library & Automatic Bookmarks

- Click **Library** in the top navigation bar to open your slide-out bookshelf.
- **Automatic Bookmarks:** Whenever you pause, exit, or close your browser, your exact word position is saved. When you reopen the document from the library, it resumes precisely where you left off.
- **Table of Contents (Chapters):** For Markdown, EPUB, and structured PDFs, press `C` or click the Chapter List icon to open the chapter drawer and jump between sections.

---

## 6. Reading Velocity & Analytics

- Click the **Bar Chart** icon in the header to view your **Reading Velocity & Analytics** dashboard.
- Tracks:
  - Total lifetime words read.
  - Total time invested.
  - Personal top speed record (WPM).
  - Average velocity across completed reading streams.
  - Scrollable history log of all completed reading sessions.

## 7. Keyboard Shortcuts Quick Reference

Press <kbd>?</kbd> at any time in the app to display this reference card:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Play / Pause reading stream |
| `←` / `→` | Step 1 word backward / forward |
| `[` / `]` | Rewind / Skip 1 sentence |
| `↑` / `↓` | Increase / Decrease speed by 25 WPM |
| `1` | Switch to Classic ORP Mode |
| `2` | Switch to Ghost Context Mode |
| `3` | Switch to Multi-Word Chunking Mode |
| `R` | Restart reading from word zero |
| `F` | Toggle vertical focal guide lines |
| `M` | Toggle auditory metronome micro-click |
| `C` | Open Table of Contents drawer |
| `?` | Open Keyboard Shortcuts modal |
| `Escape` | Close active modal or exit reader view |
